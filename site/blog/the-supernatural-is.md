## Results

| Real   | 185 | 88% |

Wow, the *vast majority* believes in the supernatural!
I honestly went into this thinking it would be the opposite.

## Demographics

Those who said *Real* were fairly mixed in every way.

Those who said *Fake* were almost entirely white people,
but fairly diverse in gender and age.
The only non-white person was a young filipino-ish woman.

## Discussions

A half dozen people asked what I meant by supernatural.
I said "anything from God to ghosts."

A few people were Christians and put Real.

I was surprised how many people included aliens in the supernatural.
It was at least 3 or 5 who stated it without being asked.
For the few who asked me if it counted,
I said no, because if they're real, they're just as natural as we are.
(I watched too much Stargate SG-1 when I was young.)

Several people said they believe in ghosts
because odd things happened that are very ghost-y.

Several people said they don't believe in ghosts,
but they believe in spirits.

Some people said they believe in alternate dimensions.

Some people said they don't believe in ghosts or spirits,
but that there's got to be more to the cosmic fabric of reality,
and that science can't explain it all.

One young man who said *Fake*
said that everything is scientific,
and if we discover something like ghosts,
they too will by definition be scientifically explainable.
And that nothing can exist outside of the realm of science.
