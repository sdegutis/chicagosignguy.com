Yesterday, I briefly mentioned that I had crashed out,
defaced my own survey, threw it away, and left early.

I am Frankenstein's monster.
I have been irreparably broken
for as long as I can remember,
though from nature or nurture
is still quite unclear to me.

In middle school, boys nicknamed me "alien"
due to my lack of understanding of people.
I have tried to make friends many times.
But something in me is incompatible with it.

A few years ago, I still had some hope left in me.
So I decided to brute force my way out of this hell.
I began to try again to form real friendships,
to learn how to be normal, and to fall in love.

Now I have nobody in my life anymore.
And every opportunity to change that,
slips through my fingers like sand,
the moment I receive it into my hand.

The drawing at the top of this page
was created a year ago, by a young woman
who I have talked to many times.
But now, she avoids even looking at me,
let along talking to me, as she passes by.

This holds true with everyone I meet.
The more I interact with them,
the more they become afraid of me.
Nothing I've ever tried has fixed this.
My every attempted solution inevitably fails.

I am Frankenstein's monster.
Every day, I curse God for making me this way,
for allowing the ruin of my life before it began.
I pray for death daily, a prayer not yet granted.

The fact that God still denies this request,
either as a reward or a punishment,
indicates that I have yet more work to do.
It's clear as day that he's waiting on *me*.

But what is it that I must first do?
What Frankenstein's monster does, of course:
destroy the ideologies that robbed me of a chance to be happy,
obliterate the lies that stole my life from the very cradle.

Like Samson destroying his captors with his dying breath,
I will pick up the pen, write until my fingers bleed,
and remove every shadow of a doubt, from every mind on earth,
that these ideologies can produce anything but rotten fruit.

But you can't fight fire with more fire.
You catch more bees with honey than vinegar.
So my book will not be a villainous rant.
Rather, I will describe the beauty I see in life,
the poetic, mystical way I view reality.

This is my only life's purpose now.
I am no longer hoping to make friends.
I am no longer hoping to fall in love.
I am no longer hoping to become normal.

I am Frankenstein's monster.
And I will have my revenge.
And it will be beautiful.
