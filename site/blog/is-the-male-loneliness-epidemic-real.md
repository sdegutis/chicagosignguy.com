## Results

| Answer | #   | %   |
| ------ | --- | --- |
| Real   | 45  | 64% |
| Fake   | 25  | 36% |

About 2/3 think it's real, 1/3 thinks it's fake.

However, of the 2/3 that thinks it's real,
a good number clearly think it's men's own fault.

## Demographics

They're color coded by chromosomes.
XX = red, XY = blue.

I thought more men would say real and women say fake,
but it turned out to be the opposite.

Most men who said it was fake seemed fairly effeminate.
