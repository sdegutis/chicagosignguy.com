# Conversations

An older woman and a younger woman who was likely her daughter,
both dressed fairly liberally, the mother clearly a hippie,
both surprised me, by being the first two to put Bad.

The older woman brought up the Cornell campus case,
saying she believes feminism has led to injustice.

The younger woman cited actual inequality in the workplace,
saying that women now have cushy, easy jobs,
whereas true equality would mean women take on more hard jobs.
Some feminists I talked to later scoffed indignantly at this idea.

Several people who put Bad refused to elaborate,
and some were clearly in too much of a hurry to.
