## Results

| Answer        | #   | %   |
| ------------- | --- | --- |
| No            | 16  | 16% |
| A little      | 7   | 7%  |
| Significantly | 77  | 78% |

## Demographics

Most people who said *No* were women.

All other answers were split fairly evenly.

## Discussions

The first woman to put *No* marked it somewhat aggressively and then left.
She seemed to be busy, but also rather passionate about the answer.
I didn't get a chance to ask her why.

One woman said she gets cat-called even when wearing pajamas.

One woman said women are still fighting for the same rights as men.

One man said there's not enough research into women's healthcare.

One woman just said "I have seven brothers."

One older woman simply said "look at our ages, we've seen a lot."
Her friend, also an older woman, said Roe v Wade was overturned.

One woman said there's a push towards more traditional roles,
such as the trad-wife movement.
She said if you're child-free, your friends would ask
when you're going to have babies,
and they almost question why you exist if you're not going to reproduce.

One black woman said, "it's an odd question, similar to is racism still alive."

One foreign woman who put *No* said,
"definitely not, it's the best time to be alive as a woman."

One woman who put *No* said "I haven't experienced any disadvantages."

One man who put *No* said there are many women CEOs.

One woman who put *Significantly* said "not until abortion is legalized."

One woman who put *Significantly* said women are objectified and sexualized,
that even from age 3 they are judged based on whether they are pretty.

One woman who put *Significantly* said, "Cornell seven."

One woman who put *A little* said "not disadvantaged, more like inconvenienced."

One man who put *Significantly* said
we need a female president and abortion rights.

One woman who put *A little* said they are paid less and have to work harder.

One man who said *No* gave an example, saying
if a man ran through the streets naked, people would say he's crazy,
but if a woman ran through the streets naked, people would assume she was robbed.

One woman who put *Significantly* cited "femicide and rapes"
and said we went backwards over the past 10 years.

One woman who put *Significantly* said
there isn't really female representation
in decisions such as abortion laws.
Her friend who had the same answer said
she and her mom do most of the work around the house,
and she can't convince her dad or brother to do more,
because they say it's not really their responsibility,
and that she can't convince her mom, who is stuck in her ways.

One young man who put *Significantly* said
women can't walk around safely at 1 am.

One woman who put *Significantly* said
his sister was raped 4 months ago.

One woman who put *Significantly* cited the wage gap.

One woman who put *No* said
"if anything, we're more advantaged."

One young woman who put *Significantly* in bold, said she doesn't know why.

One woman who put *Significantly* said,
"your rights don't change when you cross a border; mine do."

One woman who put *Significantly* said,
"simple, everyday things are harder and more dangerous."

One man who put *Significantly* cited misogyny in media, and the pay gap.

One woman who put *Significantly* said
it's not as much a problem here,
but globally it's still a real problem,
for example there is still child marriage in some places.

One woman who put *No* said,
"they're not; we can do whatever we want!"

One man who said *A little*
said he hasn't experienced it
because he lives in the suburbs
and his mom has a PhD in psychology.
