## Results

| Answers  | #   | %   |
| -------- | --- | --- |
| Innocent | 26  | 24% |
| Guilty   | 83  | 76% |

About 3/4 of people think she's Guilty,
and about 1/4 of people say she's Innocent.

## Demographics

Guilty was split 50/50 by gender,
and was perfectly diverse.
I didn't ask of them why.

Innocent was *entirely* women,
except for one black man,
who was also the only one
to refuse to answer why.

## Discussions

The first tally was for Innocent.
She said there was something about "him",
presumably the husband,
that just seemed "off".

Several people, all black,
said "she's guilt *as fuck*",
literally word for word.

A "party" bus full of women
looked at my sign and cheered.

A security guard from nearby
said he was watching people tally
and noticed it was "all white women."
I didn't notice they were white,
but he seemed to take race seriously,
so I trust his judgment on that.
After that I started watching
and noticed it was all white women
except for one asian woman.
He told me he researched it thoroughly
and strongly believes she's guilty.

At least a few people
who put Innocent said
"the husband did it."

One young woman
who put Innocent said
women's mental health
isn't taken seriously.

One young woman put Guilty at first,
saying she thinks Lindsay had mental illness,
but later came back to "erase" her answer,
and put a tally under Innocent.

One woman who put Innocent said
she saw a video of the family at a zoo
and Lindsay was juggling the kids
while the man was unconcerned and at ease.
I'm paraphrasing it to the best of my memory.

Several women differentiated
between cause and justice,
saying perhaps she did it,
but she shouldn't go to jail.

A few older women said Guilty,
which surprised me greatly.
One of them explained to me
that she watched the case closely,
and that Lindsay did it out of envy,
because her husband had another child
with someone else after her.

A young woman videoed me,
slowly, from all angles,
including getting behind me.
When I asked her why,
she said she just likes to
make men uncomfortable.
When I told her I wasn't,
she said I was lying.
I feel so sad for her.
Anyone who acts like that
can't possibly be happy.

## Songs of the Day

There were *two* songs of the day.
One for Innocent and one for Guilty.

Innocent was *Pushit* by Tool.
There's also the "Salival" version
which is calmer and more drawn out,
but just as intense and passionate.

The point of *Pushit* is that
sometimes a relationship
can be so suffocating
that the only exit
seems to be murder-suicide.
It even has the phrase
"choke this infant here before me."
In fact, this survey idea
came from me listening to it.

Guilty was *My Name is Mud* by Primus.
I've never heard any other Primus song,
but a young man told me that whole album is great,
so I'll have to check it out.
The 1994 live performance of it
where his suit is an American flag
is one of the most locked in performances
I've ever seen by any live band, ever.

The relation to *My Name is Mud* here
is because in it, he murders his friend
entirely over an unmentioned trifle,
with no remorse or even conscience.
It's admittedly a bit of a stretch,
not commenting on Lindsay that much,
but mostly I just like that song.
