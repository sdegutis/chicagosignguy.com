I have met a surprising number of women
who say that [Lindsay Clancy] is innocent.
Many of them give no evidence except that
either the husband gives them a bad feeling,
or he most likely was abusive towards her,
or that in some way was truly the monster.

I have met a surprising number of women
who say that [feminism is good] but purely because
men are inherently dangerous creatures,
who must be tamed by women into being civil.

I have seen a surprising number of women
who would choose a bear over a man
if they had to meet one in the woods,
saying they would be safer with a bear
than alone with any given random man.

I have seen a surprising number of women
who defended Amber Heard's innocence
even after Johnny Depp was awarded judgment
for her defamatory statements against him.

Several times, I have met women
who have vibed with me and I with them.
And after talking for a short while,
it soon came out that they strongly held
one or more of the above beliefs.

I don't want to be with someone
who is afraid of me,
who thinks I'm a danger,
who inherently distrusts me,
who seriously doubts I can be civil,
who believes she needs to tame me,
and who thinks she's morally superior to me.

But so far, that's every women who's flirted with me.
Maybe that's the whole problem in the first place.
Maybe I need to flirt first with women I might like.
But that's just as dangerous for me and my reputation.
What if she's one of the above?
Then my flirting will be taken as sexual harassment,
no matter how harmless, innocent, and legal it is.
Then I will be blasted on social media.

To be fair, I'm writing this a day or two
after writing an article called the [Theology of Boobs].
But I thought we were all mature adults here,
and that I could write an article about serious topics
with a silly title that no one should be alarmed at.
