This was the second survey I did today,
after a few hours of [the first one],
which was shorter and more topical.

It came about because I thought of this phrase:

> It's better to have loved and lost,
> than to have never loved at all.

We've almost all heard that phrase,
but I wondered if it was true.

## Results

|              | #   | %   |
| ------------ | --- | --- |
| Loved & Lost | 123 | 77% |
| Never Loved  | 37  | 23% |

I had to clarify to several people that
this question is *only* about romantic love.

## Discussions

Most people who said it's better to have loved and lost,
said that the positive experience outweighs the pain.

An old woman said she lost her husband to old age,
and they were married for many decades,
and that it was still worth it.

Most people who said Never Loved
simply said the pain isn't worth it.

One person said that the experience
of having loved and lost that love
builds character.

Several people did not understand the question,
and thought that "loved & lost" meant losing yourself.

(There were more answers and interesting events,
but I'm tired and just want to get this published for now.
I'll probably edit it with more details tomorrow morning.)
