The following texts were sent to me today,
regarding a brief interaction I had on Saturday
with someone who I had forgotten about.

### The survey

For context, she was a young autistic woman I met on Saturday.
I was doing one of my surveys, and she stopped to talk to me,
asking me many questions, seeming fairly excited.

We talked for maybe 45 minutes, and seemed to think a lot alike.
So I asked for her number, saying we should talk some more.
It was clearly not flirtatious or romantic, to be clear.

But after getting her number, I did bring that up,
saying that ultimately I am looking for someone
to fall in love with while remaining completely celibate.
She awkwardly declined, so I offered to delete her number.
She again said not yet, and that she will think it all over.

A few hours later, she texted me, politely saying no thanks.
She said that, in general, she now felt uncomfortable with me.
We texted for a bit, and I said I am now deleting her number,
so that, if she wanted to text me back, the ball is in her court.

She texted back a few times, and each time
we talked for a little bit, and then I deleted her number.
To be clear, not once was she ever changing her mind.
We were simply talking about socializing itself.
Most of the conversations I don't remember at all.
I just remember her being uncomfortable with me.

At one point, I gave her this website to check out,
thinking maybe she would read it
and it would put her mind at ease about me.

After the fourth or so time of her texting me, us talking,
and me deleting her number, I never heard back.

The entire thing took place within a few hours.

### The train

A few days later, I see her on the same train I take to Chicago.
Not wanting to make anything more awkward than it already was,
I ignore her entirely. That's the last I see or hear of her.
Until today.

### The texts

Today, out of nowhere,
she texts me around lunch,
with the following request:

![texts](/img/dontall.png)

I already know her reason.
I am weird, and I make her uncomfortable.
My texts were probably too unorthodox.
Ignoring her completely on the train was the wrong call.
If she had just admitted any of that,
I would have immediately acquiesced.

But the fact that she came out immediately hostile here,
treating me like a danger, or a villain with ill intent,
or someone she can't be seen with even *hypothetically*,
is so deeply hurtful, so incredibly offensive and unjust,
that this entire story *definitely* belongs in my book.

What's most ironic is that, until she sent these texts,
I had literally forgotten entirely about her,
and she wasn't even going to have a small part in my book.

All day I had been feeling defeated by life.
I kept remembering countless social faux pas
that I had made in the past few months,
and many I had made throughout my whole life.
I kept trying to figure out what was wrong with me,
why my personality seems irreparably broken.

Then for her to text like this out of nowhere,
it was just the nail in the coffin.
I am defeated.
I am too broken.
I am going to stop trying to make friends.
I will publish my book,
and live out my days in quiet isolation.

### Other people who treat me like a leper

Over the past year, I've met many people,
and some of them I've texted with.
I've deleted all their numbers.

#### Charles

Charles, who transitioned to female around 20,
and now around 30 is transitioning back to male,
and has recently begun to practice Catholicism.
He's in a band, and going to college for software.

We met in Chicago this past summer while I was living there,
and smoked and talked about theology for hours.

We then texted for months over the summer about
the struggles of being a young man in the city.

One day I texted him while in a bad mood, saying I give up on life.
Usually he would encourage me to keep going.
This time he just never texted back.
That was months ago.

#### Fionn

Fionn, a young woman who I met while doing my Awkward Smalltalk sign in early May.
We talked for an hour or two, it turned into an ad hoc date.
But at the end, I didn't kiss her, even though she gave me three chances.
It didn't occur to me until a few months later that those were invites.

Later that night, over text, I told her I was homeless
(this was during my [99 days homeless in chicago experiment] over the summer).
She replied suggesting some resources that could help me.

The only next time I saw her, I was wandering Chicago at night,
lonely and bored, audibly cursing God for making me this way.
I coincidentally saw her with some of her friends,
made it even more awkward, and left.

She never texted me back after that.

#### Prairie

Prairie, a young Jewish woman that I met while selling Terrible Advice one day.
She was a bit flirty, and so I got her number, but told her my intentions
of wanting to fall in love with someone but without sexuality at all.
We went on a few dates around Chicago, I played piano for her for a bit,
and we talked for hours and hours about everything that came to mind.
I told her I was open to the idea of loosening up on my sexual morals.
She kept texting me raunchy things, and I kept texting her likewise.

She was 19 and in college, and told me her parents were overprotective,
and kept a close eye on her and all her whereabouts, so that dating would be hard.
But she also told me not to believe everything people say.
She kept trying to get me to install Signal and only talk to her there.
A few months later, I put two and two together, and realized she had a boyfriend,
and that she was trying to cheat on him with me.

We only saw each other in person two times after the first meeting.
I pushed her away several times through texts, but she kept texting me back.
One of those times, she offered me oral sex if I came to Skokie, which I declined.
Some days I hate that Catholicism is true.

One day I pushed her away permanently, saying "genuinely, **** you, lose my number."
It was not because of anything she had done wrong or offensive,
but purely because I was so utterly lonely, and she had a reasonably busy life.
I was simply hurting very badly by how few texts I get from anyone, ever.

This was mid summer.

#### Rock concert girl

Jacylin? Jacinta? I forget her name, but something like that.
A young black woman who was on break from work, who I flirted with a bit,
who asked me to try to spell her name and talked about random stuff with me.

I got her number, and we texted quite a few times that evening.
She was at a friend's rock concert managing it for her, and doodling some art,
and sent me what she was drawing, and I sent her some of what I drew.

After that day, I apparently texted her far too much,
and I got completely radio silence, until one day.
I had crashed out that day and sent all 7 or so people the same text,
saying that I'm tired of failing to figure out how to socialize.

She said I should be a man and stop whining like a little bitch.
She also told me I was texting too much, and to lose her number.

#### Bri

Bri, a young professional welder with colored hair,
who I met while doing one of my money making signs.
She seemed cool and we vibed quite well I thought.
I got her number and tried to schedule hanging out.

After a week or something, she texted back, saying that
she didn't text back for so long because she wasn't sure
how to ask whether I was romantically interested or not,
and telling me up front that she has a boyfriend.

I also wasn't sure what my intentions were at the time.
I still thought she was cool and said we should hang out,
and said her boyfriend's probably cool and he should come too.

We texted a lot and scheduled several times to hang out,
but each time they fell through, always on her end.

I sent her the same crashout text I sent the other woman,
and I never heard back from her again after that.

#### Bridge guy

A young man, I don't remember his name.
It was about midnight one day this summer,
and I was walking back to where I sleep
after selling Terrible Advice by the bars.

I saw him and his friend on the bridge,
and in a moment of hope to find friends,
I stopped to talk to them about the band Tool.
We talked about several things for ten minutes,
and I told them I'm trying to make new friends,
and I got his number,
and we agreed to get drinks some time.

A day or a few days later,
I texted him saying it was a mistake,
that I can't possibly make friends,
and that I don't really know how to socialize,
and that I would delete his number.

But he texted me back, trying to be encouraging,
saying that it's hard for men out there,
and that he's still down to hang out and get drinks.
I insisted that it's hopeless for me.

#### Evangelist killer

I forget her name, but I met her about a year ago.

A young Protestant came up to me,
and was doing his Protestant thing,
trying to evangelize me
and everyone who came up to me.

Then these two black girls came up to me,
and I was relatively annoyed at this guy's naivety,
so while he was trying to evangelize them,
I pushed him with the really hard questions
about life, the ones he even struggles with.

He kept trying to debate us,
and you could see his eyes light up
when he was asked a question
that he had memorized an answer for.
It was so annoying and sad.

Anyway, one of the young black women
was crushing him in this little "debate"
and I had gained a lot of respect for her.
I asked her to help light my cigar
while her friend talked with him,
and she used her hands as a wind shield for me.

Anyway, we talked a bit, and I got her number.
I called her the "evangelist killer"
because of how she demolished his arguments.

After I posted that article,
her friend "D" commented on it,
back when this website had comments.

We texted a few times, she said she was likely autistic,
and I asked her quite a lot about that,
but I don't remember much else we talked about.
I do remember that one time I tried to impress her
by telling her about a piano song I was learning.

I tried to ask her out on a date
but she said that I'm not her type.
I asked if she wanted to just hang out,
and she said she had moved for work.

I eventually became very despairing again,
and it came through in my texts.
She stopped replying shortly after that.

This was last winter,
so I'm likely forgetting a lot of details,
and possibly misremembering what I do remember.

I've seen her friend "D" pass by at least once.
She just walks by awkwardly and avoids me,
and even avoids making eye contact with me.

#### Art store girls

Now I hate going into Blick.

#### Blonde girl

About a month ago, a bleach blonde did one of my surveys.
Shortly after, I posted an article that included my phone number.
She was the only one to text me,
and she said she texted me because it sounded like a suicide note.

I lied by saying it had nothing to do with me dying young.
I asked if she wanted me to delete her number, and she said no.
We texted for a few days about life in general.
It was never romantic or meant to be, in either direction.
I don't remember any particulars that we talked about.

But at some point we brought up the question,
would you rather be alone in the woods with a man or bear.
Despite me not answering, she figured my answer was bear,
and seemingly blocked me, as I never heard from her again.

#### Addi

Addi, I met her about two months ago.
A young woman who lives in the same town as me.
I saw her pretending to be homeless in Chicago,
selling drawings on the sidewalk as she drew more.
She told me she makes at least $100 per day doing it.

We talked a bit, and somehow it turned into flirting.
She said she's poly and lives with her main.
We bought beer and talked on the train back here.

We texted a bit, and made plans to hook up on Monday,
and on Monday morning I promptly sabotaged it all,
and immediately got on the train to Chicago.
Sometimes I really hate being Catholic.

Anyway, after a few days of texting,
she texted saying we should just be friends.
I told her I still wanted to hang out,
but then I changed my mind.
I assume she lost my number after this.

A few weeks later I saw her in the same spot.
Not knowing what to do, I gave her a dollar.
It was, I suppose, meant to be an insult,
considering she's pretending to be homeless.

A few months later I saw her again in the same spot.
We had a conversation about our opposing ideologies,
until I had to leave to catch the train.
I asked for her number and she accepted,
but only on the condition it was platonic.
I declined.

#### Efrend

Efrend, a young Mexican Catholic man and gym bro.

But he's the only person

#### The girl from the story

In a sample [prologue](/blog/prologue.html) I wrote,
which I probably won't be including in my book,
I told a true story that happened to me about 4 years ago.
What I left out is what happened afterward.

First, I got her number shortly after meeting her,
before the events of that story happened.

That night, afters she left, I sent her a photo of Dick Cheney,
telling her that it was a "dick pic."
She sent me a photo of the dinner she was making.
I forget what else we said that night,
but we were still vibing over text.

The next day I was feeling sad that she would be moving soon.
So I texted asking if there's any last thing she wanted to tell me,
since we would never be able to see each other ever again.
She texted back saying "that's weird as fuck" and "lose my number".
It didn't occur to me until years later that people can move or travel.

### My conclusion

I'm hopelessly stupid.
