I started doing these surveys a year ago.
Originally, they were not for my book.
I did them because I had nothing else to do.

I had just lost my family and home to divorce.
I had just lost my last software client to AI.
It was Sunday, and I felt utterly alone.

The first survey I remember doing was "how alone do you feel?"
I just wanted to interact with someone, *anyone*.
These surveys were the only reason I wasn't alone on my last birthday (Nov 2).

It seems I think differently than everyone else.
This makes it impossible to make friends.
But for some reason, I just kept on trying.

While doing my surveys, I have met many people.
I've had many great, interesting conversations.
But I've made no lasting friendships in the process.

By now, I've gotten used to the extreme isolation.
I don't really want to talk to anyone anymore.
The surveys are now just a painful reminder that I'm all alone.

So I'll do one more survey, just to say goodbye.
I'm torn between two potential questions,
"can you earn love?" and "do you believe in destiny?"

I'm glad someone suggested the destiny question.
I've been thinking about it constantly for years.
Part of me wonders if I'm cursed to isolation.

But I also realized I've been trying to *deserve* love.
Yet when I see couples, or friend hanging out,
it's clear that they're there because they *want* to be.

While I'm writing this, I'm listening
to [Shepherd] by Joep Beving.
A year ago, I learned half of it on piano.
But I have no one to play it for.
So why learn the rest?
