I did not plan to do another survey today.
I only came to Chicago to go to Mass.
When I got there, it was already 3pm,
and I had to leave by shortly before 5pm.

But I had a cigar, and time to kill,
so I bought a sign, figuring why not.
I had two topics left in my notes,
and one was "is anyone irredeemable?"

About 3/4 think some people are beyond hope.

## Discussions

A few people who put *No* said "Trump" or "politicians."

One person who put *No* said "rapists."

A few people who put *No* simply said "Jesus,"
or "I'm a Christian." For a few of them,
I tried to clarify that I meant in *this* life,
and not simply that a person can go to heaven.
But it was hardly worth saying for every person.

There was a discussion about whether
redemption means being rehabilitated
or paying off a debt owed to society.
